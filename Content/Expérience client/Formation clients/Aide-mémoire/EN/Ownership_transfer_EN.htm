BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Have these items ready. Set aside 10 to 15 quiet minutes.

What you need

Where to find it

Starlink activation email

In your inbox, sender Starlink “no-reply@starlink.com”, subject “Starlink Transfer: Ready to Activate”. Remember to check your spam folder.

Starlink Kit Number

On the label of your antenna or in the Loki Coach delivery folder. Keep it in a safe place: you will need it again if you ever swap hardware or contact Starlink support.

Email address

Your personal address. It becomes your login and will receive your invoices.

Phone number

Your mobile or home line. Used for security verification.

Service address

Your primary residence. It determines the country of service.

Credit card

To pay for your chosen plan. You enter your card details yourself on the Starlink website.

Desc.

How to do it

Verification

1

Open the Starlink email

In your inbox, open the email from Starlink titled “Ready to Activate”. Click the “Activate Starlink” button.

If you cannot find this email, go to step 2.

The Starlink site opens directly on the activation page. You can skip to step 4.

2

Open the Starlink website

On a computer or smartphone, open starlink.com.

The Starlink home page opens.

3

Choose to activate existing hardware

Click “Activate Existing Hardware”.

An account creation form appears.

4

Select your country

Choose 🇨🇦 Canada or 🇺🇸 United States, depending on your service address.

The country sets your service area and available plans.

5

Enter your details

Enter your name, email, phone number and service address.

Your personal account is created. You receive a confirmation email.

6

Enter the Kit Number

Enter the Kit Number of your antenna.

Your installed antenna is linked to your account.

7

Choose your service plan

Select the Starlink plan that fits your usage (mobile, residential, etc.).

The monthly fee is displayed.

8

Confirm payment

Enter your payment method and confirm activation.

Service starts. Billing is now in your name.

 

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

If a step blocks you, locate the symptom in the table below.

Symptom

Likely cause

What to do

You cannot find the Starlink email “Ready to Activate”.

Email flagged as spam, deleted, or never received (wrong address provided).

Check your spam folder. If the email cannot be found, go to step 2 of the procedure and continue without it.

The site rejects the Kit Number.

Typing error, or the kit is already linked to another account.

Double-check digit by digit. If it still fails, call your Loki Coach specialist.

The country shown is not yours.

Service address entered for a different country.

Go back to step 3 and select the country of your primary residence.

No plans appear.

Starlink service not available at the address provided.

Verify the address. If the issue remains, contact Starlink support.

Payment is declined.

Card not enabled for international payments, or limit reached.

Try another card or contact your bank. Payment goes directly to Starlink.

You cannot find your Kit Number.

Label removed or delivery folder misplaced.

Call your Loki Coach specialist. They can retrieve the number tied to your coach.

 

The 4 common pitfalls to avoid

1.  Clicking “Order Now” instead of “Activate Existing Hardware”. You would buy a second antenna. Your hardware is already installed: always pick “Activate Existing Hardware”.

2.  Selecting the wrong country. Service will not work in your area. Verify before confirming.

3.  Using the email of a relative or the dealer. The account must be in the owner’s name — yours. Otherwise you cannot change the plan or contact support in your own name.

4.  Postponing activation. Until the system is activated in your name, service will not work, even though the antenna is installed.

 

 

 

When to call support — and who

Call Starlink if:  service is activated but you face a connection, billing or plan issue.
