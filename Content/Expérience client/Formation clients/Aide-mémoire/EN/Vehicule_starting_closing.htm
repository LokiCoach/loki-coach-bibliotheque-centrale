The action

How to do it

Check

Check the power supply

Confirm that the coach's cable is properly connected to the site's 50-amp outlet.

The power indicator light is on..

Unlock

On the exterior keypad, enter your 4-digit code, then press the 3/4 key..

Main door and storage bays unlocked.

At the entrance, press the ALLbutton. All the lights turn on at once.

✓  Shutdown — before departure

N°

The action

How to do it

Check

1

Turn off the water pump

Depuis n'importe quel écran ou l'écran mobile (onglet Plomberie), désactivez Pump.

No more pump noise in the background.

2

Tidy up the interior

Le coach doit retrouver l'état dans lequel il vous a été remis : propre, rangé, sans objet personnel oublié.

No objects on the surfaces or seats.

3

Motorized beds in the raised position.

Before closing the slide-outs, always raise the motorized beds first. Otherwise, the walls will not be able to close.

The beds are in the fully raised position.

4

Rentrer les extensions

From the Garmin tablet (with the ignition on), retract the slide-outs. Check that there are no obstacles inside the vehicle.

The beds are in the fully raised position.

5

Reconnect if unplugged.

If the 50-amp cable was disconnected during the event, reconnect it to the outlet before leaving the premises.

Power indicator light on again.

6

Lock

On the exterior keypad, press the 9/0 key. The main door and storage bays lock together.

An audible signal confirms the locking.

 

 

⚠  Common pitfalls

Situation encountered

The right response

The slide-outs don't move when I press the button on the tablet.

The ignition is probably not on. Turn the vehicle's start button to the ON position, without starting the engine.

The water isn't flowing even though I activated Pump.

Check that the fresh water tanks are filled. Otherwise, wait 30 seconds: the pump may take a moment to prime itself.

I close the slide-outs and a lockout is triggered.

Immediately stop the operation. A motorized bed has probably been left in the lowered position. Raise it, then try again.

My 4-digit code no longer works.

First, check that the exterior keypad lights up. If the problem persists, call Customer Experience.

☎  When to call Customer Experience

You stop everything and call if:

  • a slide-out makes an unusual noise or gets stuck during the operation ;

  • a red light or an audible alarm appears on the Garmin tablet or a screen ;

  • you smell gas or burning, or see smoke ;

  • the 4-digit code no longer works; ;

  • you have any doubt whatsoever about the procedure to follow.
